# SOVA-OSS trademark and fork-naming policy

- **Policy version:** 1.0
- **Effective date:** 2026-07-29
- **Contact:** [gautam@truscor.org](mailto:gautam@truscor.org)

## Marks

TRUSCOR claims the following project marks:

- the word mark **SOVA-OSS™**;
- the equivalent project stylizations **SOVA OSS** and **S.O.V.A.**;
- the official SOVA owl logo distributed by this project.

The selected canonical trademark name is **SOVA-OSS**.

This policy does not claim that a mark is registered in any particular jurisdiction. Do not use the ® symbol unless TRUSCOR publishes verified registration details permitting it.

This is a project brand-governance decision, not a legal clearance opinion or registration. Before filing or a promoted executable release, conduct a class-specific clearance search in India and every intended market, then have qualified trademark counsel confirm ownership, filing classes, and registrability.

The Apache License 2.0 grants copyright and patent permissions for the licensed work. It does not grant trademark rights beyond reasonable and customary use describing the work's origin and reproducing `NOTICE`.

## Purpose

The code may be used, modified, forked, and sold under Apache-2.0. The marks identify the official project and protect users from confusing an unofficial modification with a TRUSCOR-maintained release.

This policy controls branding, not the technical freedom granted by the licence.

## Uses that do not require permission

### Accurate reference

You may use the word mark to truthfully identify the official project:

- “uses SOVA-OSS”;
- “compatible with SOVA-OSS”;
- “implements the `.sova` specification”;
- “based on SOVA-OSS”;
- “a fork of SOVA-OSS”;
- “results produced with SOVA-OSS version X.”

Use only as much of the mark as necessary and do not imply endorsement, certification, partnership, or affiliation.

### Verbatim redistribution

You may identify a byte-for-byte, unmodified official release as **SOVA-OSS** when:

- the release came from an official project channel;
- signatures and release metadata are preserved;
- `LICENSE` and `NOTICE` are preserved;
- you clearly identify yourself as the distributor;
- you do not imply that TRUSCOR provides your support, warranty, service, or modified packaging.

Rebuilding from an unmodified official source tag may be described as an “unofficial build of SOVA-OSS” if the build source, tag, modifications, and non-affiliation are clear.

### Publications and education

You may use SOVA-OSS in the title or text of papers, books, articles, courses, talks, benchmarks, and tutorials when the use describes the project accurately.

Please:

- use the citation metadata in `CITATION.cff`;
- identify SOVA-OSS as a TRUSCOR project led by Gautam R. Patil;
- link the official repository;
- state that SOVA-OSS is a trademark of TRUSCOR;
- avoid implying that TRUSCOR reviewed or endorsed the publication.

### Plugins, integrations, and compatibility

A third-party product may use a form such as:

- “Example Adapter for SOVA-OSS”;
- “Example, compatible with SOVA-OSS”;
- “Example, powered by SOVA-OSS.”

The third party's own name must be the dominant brand. The product must include a visible non-endorsement statement and must not use the official owl logo without written permission.

## Modified forks must use a different primary name

You may modify and distribute the code under Apache-2.0, but a materially modified distribution must use a distinct primary product name and visual identity.

Acceptable:

> **Nightwatch** — a fork of SOVA-OSS<br>
> Based on SOVA-OSS, originally developed by Gautam R. Patil and TRUSCOR.<br>
> Nightwatch is independent and is not endorsed by TRUSCOR.

Not acceptable without written permission:

- SOVA-OSS Pro;
- SOVA Enterprise;
- SOVA Secure;
- SOVA-OSS Community Edition;
- SOVA Next;
- a confusing misspelling or transliteration of SOVA-OSS;
- the official owl logo or a confusingly similar owl logo as the fork's primary mark;
- a company, package, organization, domain, application, hosted service, or social account branded as though it were an official SOVA-OSS offering.

A fork may retain technical compatibility strings, protocol identifiers, historical attribution, and truthful statements of origin. It must not rewrite protocol or artifact identifiers solely to conceal origin.

## Hosted and commercial services

Commercial use is permitted by Apache-2.0.

A service built from SOVA OSS may say “based on SOVA-OSS” or “powered by SOVA-OSS,” subject to this policy. It may not call the service **SOVA-OSS**, use the official logo as its own, or imply that its results are TRUSCOR-issued, independently attested, certified, or endorsed.

## Domains, organizations, and package names

Without written permission, do not register or use a name whose dominant element is SOVA-OSS, SOVA OSS, S.O.V.A., or a confusingly similar variant for:

- a domain or subdomain;
- a source-control organization;
- a company or legal entity;
- a package namespace;
- an application-store listing;
- a conference, certification, training programme, or hosted service.

Truthful descriptive paths and metadata are allowed, such as an article slug containing `sova-oss` or a package description stating compatibility.

## Official logo

You may display an unmodified official owl logo:

- to link to the official repository;
- in accurate commentary, news, reviews, or academic discussion;
- in a factual dependency or compatibility list.

Do not use it as the primary logo of a fork, service, company, certification, merchandise line, or event without written permission.

Copyright permission in the logo does not override trademark law or this source-identification policy.

## No certification or endorsement

No trademark use may imply that:

- TRUSCOR reviewed or approved a fork;
- a SOVA OSS self-assessment is a TRUSCOR attestation;
- a product is an official SOVA-OSS release when it is not;
- TRUSCOR sponsors a company, paper, benchmark, course, service, or event when it does not.

## Permission requests and enforcement

Send requests to [gautam@truscor.org](mailto:gautam@truscor.org) with:

- the proposed name or logo use;
- screenshots or mockups;
- distribution channels;
- whether the use is commercial;
- the requested duration and territory.

TRUSCOR may ask a user to correct confusing branding before taking further action. Nothing in this policy limits nominative fair use, commentary, criticism, or other rights available under applicable law.

This policy should be reviewed by qualified trademark counsel before the first promoted executable release and after any registration is obtained.
